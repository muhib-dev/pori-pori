1পড়ি পড়ি — "Reading Road" redesign
One world, one friend, every tap answers back.
The step list becomes Reading Road — a single winding path the child's avatar walks up. Each step is a landmark; locked ones are hollow with a small cloud cap and the road ahead stays faded. Bulbul, a round sun-coloured bird, says every sound with the child and reacts to everything. Every game gets a story skin: feed the frog, build the cat's house, load the word train, slide to blend.
ASSUMPTIONS
• Tokens kept: ink, sun, tangerine, grass, berry, bubblegum, sky. Proposed adds: paper, card, ground (surfaces).
• Mobile 390×844 is the main frame; tablet shown in landscape for the two layouts that change most.
• Emoji are stand-ins for content art and stickers; swap for SVG later.
• Grown-ups lock is deliberately small (52px) and quiet so kids don't hit it.

MOTION PRINCIPLES
Every tap answers — sound + 90ms squash, always.
Fast joy — feedback ≤ 600ms, never blocks the next tap.
Soft no — wiggle, never shake-to-scold; nothing is taken away.
Breathe — 1.2s float breather between rounds; no timers, nothing flashes > 3Hz.
Reduced motion — everything becomes a 200ms fade (toggle in Tweaks).

How the map works
• Avatar stands on the next landmark; it glows and the road behind it gets a dotted "walked" line.
• Done landmarks show their best stars. The road ahead is faded and dashed; locked landmarks are hollow with a small cloud cap — tapping one puffs the cloud and Bulbul points back to the glowing node.
• Big Play always resumes the next lesson — the one-tap path for the youngest.
• Tap Bulbul's bubble to hear "Let's read!". Sticker book top-right, grown-ups lock tucked bottom-right.

Install & offline
• Install sheet shows only after a lesson completes (a happy moment), at most once a week. "Add" triggers the native prompt; on iOS the sheet swaps the arrow for the share → add-to-home picture.
• Offline pill reads "works without internet" — a reassurance, not a warning. Everything stays playable; it fades after 4s.
